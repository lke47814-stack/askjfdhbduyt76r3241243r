Paste My Translation

Start here. Paste your own translation and press Format / Import My Translation. The app now rejects loose prose that has no verse markers, so accidental notes or malformed files do not become a broken Bible layer.
Accepted: superscript verse numbers like ¹, ², ¹⁸; normal numbered lines; full reference lines; commentary-aware blocks. Bold is preserved from **markdown**, typed HTML like <b>bold</b>, or rich text pasted from a document.
¹ The record of the generation... ² Abraham sired Isaac... ³ and Judah... 1 In the beginning God created... 2 And the earth was... Matthew 1:1 **The record** of the generation... Matthew 1:2 Abraham sired Isaac... # Matthew 1:1 Text: **The record** of the generation... Commentary: My note on this verse. > Another commentary paragraph.
Main import is strict: every imported verse must be marked by a superscript number, normal number, reference, or # Book 1:1 block.
