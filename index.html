Settings

Formal export, writing, front matter, and note controls.

General writing controls

These affect how the editor organizes and displays your material.
Dark theme
Saved automatically and remembered after reload.
Writing mode
Universal is normal. Scripture and verse-by-verse modes organize by Bible references. The other modes add structured writing templates.
Auto-title from reference
In Scripture Mode, blank titles become the verse reference.
Scripture export sorting
Sorts export by biblical book/chapter/verse when possible.

Export appearance

These options affect Book HTML, PDF Preview, Download PDF, and folder exports.
Export style
Changes the visual style of exported books and PDFs.
Show entry titles in exports
Turn this off when the title repeats the reference or passage heading.
Show entry metadata in exports
Controls the small reference / folder / status line under each title.
Show Draft / Final / Published status in exports
Turn this off to remove Draft, Final, Finished, Published, etc. from PDF output.
For a clean book/PDF: turn off entry titles if they repeat your references, and turn off status labels when you do not want Draft / Finished language in the manuscript.

Book front matter

Used at the beginning of full-book and folder exports.

Footnotes and endnotes

Controls how note material is placed when exported.
Footnote placement
Footnotes remain directly beneath each entry so the linked marker stays near the note.
Endnote / citation placement
Choose where citation-style notes are collected in exported books and PDFs.
Scripture references inside footnotes and endnotes, such as John 3:16, Psalm 51, or Ruth 1-4, are automatically linked to Bible Gateway NKJV + YLT.
